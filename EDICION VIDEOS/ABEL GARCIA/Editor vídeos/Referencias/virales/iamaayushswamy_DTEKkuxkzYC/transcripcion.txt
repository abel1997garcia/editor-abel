Hashtags. Not important. They died over a year ago. SEO is king. Reposting reels to your stories. Not important. It'll actually kill your story views. Having one consistent person in your videos. Very important, especially for a brand. If you have multiple employees, it's better to pick one person because you can build a lot more trust and authority with one single face than changing it up all around all the time. Using outlier hooks. Very important. Just don't reinvent the wheel. You'll pop off faster. Scripts. Very important. Unless you use AI, then your scripts are going to be trash. Just do them yourself. Trends. I wouldn't make them your main content strategy, but they can be helpful if you throw them in here and there. Educational content. Very important, especially if you use your personal lived experience because it will really reinforce your authority and your expertise and what you've actually gone through. Entertainment content. Not important. Unless you're an influencer, but I mean, we're trying to make money here. Trending audios. Not important. I thought those died over a year ago too. Posting time. Not important. I've proven it time and time again. If a video is good, it's going to pop off. You can post it anytime as long as the video is good. Posting consistently. Very important. Video editing. Very important, but it's based on making it viewer-depictable. So just don't over-edit it. Make sure you just enhance the video.
