Your emotions are your teachers, not your enemies, not your weaknesses, not evidence that something is wrong with you. Your teachers. Anger is not there to embarrass you. It's showing you exactly where a boundary has been crossed that matters deeply to your soul. Jealousy is not pettiness. It's a signpost pointing directly to something that you desire and haven't yet given yourself permission to pursue. Shame is not your identity. It's an invitation to find the places in you that are still waiting to be accepted, held, and healed. Fear is not a stop sign. It's often standing directly in front of the thing that you were most powerfully designed to do. We were taught to sort our emotions into acceptable and unacceptable, to perform the good ones, and to suppress the uncomfortable. To smile through the grief and push past the anxiety and pretend that the rage was never there. And we wonder why we feel disconnected from ourselves. Every emotion that you have ever tried to outrun was carrying a message. So give yourself permission to feel it. Sit with it long enough to hear what it's saying. The wisdom on the other side of your most uncomfortable feelings is exactly what your greatest life is worth. Thank you. Is waiting for. I love you.
