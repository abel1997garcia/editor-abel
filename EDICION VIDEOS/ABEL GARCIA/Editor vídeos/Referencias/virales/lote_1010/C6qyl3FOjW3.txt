Your anxiety isn't the problem. Your sadness, anger, fear, grief, the emotion isn't the problem. The problem is your resistance to feeling it. See, we can't selectively numb our emotions. If we numb to the fear, numb to the sadness, if we push it all away, we limit our capacity to feel anything, including joy, love, creativity, passion, all the things that we actually want to feel. So if the problem is resistance, then the solution is acceptance. Make room for all the parts of yourself to exist. Meet every emotion with patience, warmth, and understanding because you can't access the peace that you want until you expand your capacity to feel it all.
