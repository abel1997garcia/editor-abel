Overexplaining is a trauma response. I'm a psychologist and I'm here to explain you why this happens. First of all, when there was no one around to listen to our needs and feelings as children, we learned to hide them. And now it feels uncomfortable to talk about them to others. We even feel guilty for having needs. Hence the overexplaining. Secondly, when we were made to feel responsible for other people's feelings, we developed the mindset that says my needs hurt others. Now, as adults, we over explain our needs in an attempt to make sure that no one gets hurt. Does it make sense now why overexplaining is a trauma response?
