The influencer era is dying. Brands are using AI to promote their products and people are sick and tired of hearing codes being pushed left and right. And let's not forget, they're also sick of seeing your perfectly curated highlight reel of a life. No one's buying your shit because not only are they having a hard time resonating with you, you're also not giving them a reason to care. In this day and age of modern technology, people are not looking for the next Beyonce to follow. They're looking for connection, a place where they feel like they belong. This is why we're seeing a rise in run clubs, in-person retreats, and online communities. I actually came across this business brand called Roll Boys on Instagram. They sell cinnamon rolls, okay? But what makes their business and their brand stand out is the story behind it. They grew their account sharing everything behind the scenes on how the owner decided to build this business he knows nothing about, showing all his mistakes, his failures, all the bad days, the good times too. And this... This built a connection with their audience, rooting for them to actually be successful. And this right here explains why they have a line of people out the door before they even open, frothing at their mouths for just a cinnamon roll. You can get cinnamon rolls anywhere, but people choose Roll Boys because of their story, because of the community and the movement that they've built. This is a good example of good branding. If you want to build a brand that stands the test of time, start building your community. Share insights on why you decided to build your personal brand. What's your compelling message? What's your contrarian belief? What gap are you filling in your industry right now? Give people a reason to rally behind you. People's attention spans are short, but what makes them stop scrolling is a compelling story that humanizes you and the movement that you're building.
