So I went a bit viral last week. I had about 400 followers and now I have somewhere over 5,000, which is both amazing and honestly quite overwhelming. I feel a bit like I've got stage fright and I just went mute for a few days. I only started this account about four months ago and a few people have asked me how I think it's growing so quickly. So I thought I'd share three things that I think are helping in case it's useful to you. Number one, I just started. I didn't have a grand plan, an end goal. I didn't really know what I was going to share and what I wasn't. I just started seeing what felt comfortable, what felt good and what I was interested in making. It's evolved from there. Number two, and I think this was a game changer, I started using trial reels. Love it or hate it, the hook matters and the first few seconds of your content make a massive difference to who's going to stay around and watch it. So on trial reels, you can essentially post the same reel but with a different opening line or a different hook. One trial reel alone that I posted ended up having 130,000 views versus the one on the grid that was like 10,000 views. That reel alone brought in 2,000 followers. Number three, I added colour. I just sat in front of these bookshelves one day and they ended up being my best performing reel. I started using trial reels, even though the subjects were quite different. So I think people see a visual hook as much as they hear one. I don't know if I'll keep growing or if it'll stop here, but I'll share my learnings as I go and hopefully it helps.
