I have what I call the brand journey framework. I like to reverse engineer every single thing that I want to do from the outcome I desire to today. It's four simple questions. So the first question that we ask is, why are we building this brand? What are we wanting to make happen? And then from there, it's what would I have to be known for in order for that outcome to occur? What do people need to know about me in order for this to happen? And then the next question you want to ask yourself is, what do I have to do? To be known for something, you got to do shit. These are the actions that are necessary. But then the next thing, oftentimes for most people is, what would I have to learn in order to do the things necessary to be known for this thing to get the outcome I want? Because oftentimes the things that are necessary to do, you might not know how to right now.
