There's the 70-20-10 framework. 70% of the content you post should be shit you know is going to work. 20% should be iterations on that 70%. 10% should be shit you have no idea and have never done. Most people get stuck on the 70%. A select few will do the 20. Very few do the 10. Because the 10 is ambiguous. There is no data to back it. But I believe that's where true innovation occurs. And so if you do that, you will be successful at innovating and being a creator that others copy and emulate.
