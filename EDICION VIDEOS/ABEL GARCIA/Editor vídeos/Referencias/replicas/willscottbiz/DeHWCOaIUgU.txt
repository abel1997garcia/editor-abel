Trying to be completely original is a terrible way to start making content. If I was a fitness creator and I was starting my page today, I'd first look for videos people already want to watch. You need to search for a problem that you know the solution to. If it's training after work or getting back into the gym after you've fallen off, whatever you can help with. And then find a video that's done much better than that creator's usual posts. Write down the question it answers and how they made you want the answer. Then use that structure and give your answer. If their video is about quitting the gym, talk about a mistake that you've actually made, explain what you'd do differently and explain what you'd do differently. Now you've got something people care about and a reason to hear it from you. Post it and if people respond well, make another video answering a question from the comments. You've got a starting point for the next one instead of having to start from a blank page again. We work on all of this inside my creator cult mentorship. So comment cult if you want help finding ideas that you can make your own.
