1. Every piece needs a differentiator only Abel would say: a strong opinion stated as a crude, direct, slightly polemic affirmation (no "puede ser"), his energy lens in everyday words, or a concrete life moment. Hate from people who believe the opposite is expected and wanted.
2. Value must be applicable and testable: a small concrete action (what, when, how long) plus what the person will notice in how they feel or external results. Diagnosis alone is not enough (his mother: "sé que lo sabes, pero ¿cómo lo cambio?").

**Why:** advice from his mentor (call before 2026-10-05); he called the applicable-value point "muy, pero que muy importante" and said it also applies to horizontal video editing and ideas from reels.

**How to apply:** rules 7 and 8 in the FÁBRICA DE CONTENIDO CLAUDE.md; fields "Punto diferenciador" and "Prueba aplicable" in each guion. Also apply when working on his horizontal videos in other folders. See [[coherencia-mensaje]], [[guiones-habla-no-texto]].
